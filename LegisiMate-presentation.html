01 / Why this exists

A cheque is not a complete case.

The difficult part often comes before an argument: knowing what is missing, what the dates mean, and what to ask next.

"I have the cheque. What else do I need to bring?"
Before / Information without structure

A folder full of uncertainties.

  • A memo date mistaken for the date it was received.
  • Invoices and disputed delivery records mixed together.
  • A general AI answer with no inspectable source.
After / A better professional conversation

A file with the right questions.

  • Known dates separated from unknown dates.
  • The strongest opposing arguments rehearsed.
  • A brief linking preparation steps to source evidence.

Reduce the preparation barrier.

Plain-language steps before complex legal choices.

Make the basis inspectable.

Source text, tool results and missing evidence stay visible.

Keep people in the decision.

Professional review and voluntary resolution, not automated legal action.

The impact goal is informed access to preparation, not a claimed reduction in court pendency.

02 / The whole application in one journey

From scattered papers
to a preparation brief.

The same facts travel through every stage. A missing fact does not become true just because the AI needs an answer.

Dates are calculated by code. Research actions and arguments are generated by the model. Source quotations are resolved by the server.

Input facts remain the same across all three argument paths.

04 / Generative AI + agentic workflow + RAG

A research desk, not a black box.

The agent chooses a bounded research action. Local retrieval returns source records. A passage ID binds a quotation to its text.

    Implementation, without the mystery

    The current build uses typed action selection, not native provider function calling. Its tools are statute search, precedent search, authority reads and deadline checks. Research selection is bounded to four model decisions. Default hearings allow 72 model calls; the maximum depth and review-panel settings allow 93.

    RAG is local SQLite FTS5/BM25 retrieval with court-hierarchy and section-priority adjustments. It is not a semantic vector index or a comprehensive good-law citator.

    05 / A rehearsal, not a verdict generator

    One case. Three full hearings.
    No preset winner.

    Nine participants. Openings, parties, witness examination, cross-examination, two rebuttal cycles and a final bench assessment. Default: 22 statements per path, 66 in total.

    Same case facts. Fresh conversation for every path.
    Your position

    Notice & service

    Illustrative excerpts, not live testimony. Missing witness material stays missing. An optional 0-3-person educational panel adds review only, not an Indian jury or judicial authority.

    The Magistrate role identifies support, contest and missing evidence. It does not predict a real ruling.

    10 / Model feasibility / Research dated 17 October 2026

    Fast decisions are not legal reasoning.

    Jev and Laya can answer bounded questions. Neither is a drop-in replacement for the open-ended arguments and brief the product currently generates.

    Proposed routing contract / Policy illustration

    Give a small decision the right job.

    No Jev/Laya model runs here. This is the proposed control policy, not a measured model result.

    Generation remains.Research queries, path questions, arguments and brief prose still need a generative model.
    Code stays deterministic.Date arithmetic and source/passage pairing are cheaper and more exact in code.
    Confidence is scoped.Validate routing accuracy and calibration on local labels and languages. Never relabel it as a chance of winning.
    No legal-domain Jev/Laya benchmark or local inference measurement has been run for LegisiMate.

    11 / International utility / Expansion proposal

    Every country with a constitution.
    Each jurisdiction on its own terms.

    The goal covers constitutions, legislation, regulations and case law worldwide. The demo uses only a selected Indian-law slice. Each country needs authoritative sources, versioned rules, language support and local evaluation.

    1 / Confirm rightsPublisher, access terms, privacy and permitted reuse.
    2 / Preserve structureNative XML where available; reviewed parsing otherwise.
    3 / Version the packCountry, authority, language, effective dates and citations.

    The strongest outcome today is not "you will win." It is "you know what to prepare, what to question, and why."

    Why isn't this just another chatbot?

    Calendar maths is deterministic, the research loop chooses tools, each argument path starts independently, and source-specific passage IDs resolve to exact text. Those contracts are distinct from generated prose.

    Does a matched quotation mean the legal advice is right?

    No. It establishes text provenance only. Interpretation, precedent treatment, jurisdiction and application to the facts still need qualified review.

    What happens to case information?

    The live product sends consented facts to the configured provider and saves plaintext case records locally. Local deletion does not erase provider copies or downloaded exports. Use anonymised inputs.

    What is the Google Cloud & Gemini compatibility status?

    The repository ships a native Gemini / Vertex AI structured adapter (GEMINI_API_KEY and GOOGLE_CLOUD_PROJECT), Cloud Run Dockerfile and cloudbuild.yaml, Firebase Hosting rewrite config, and pre-seeded showcase casework so judges can inspect a complete verified case immediately.
